About this service

Some days are simply busier than others — a full house at lunch, a weekend rush, a big party booking. A KvikStaff waiter gives you extra serving hands for exactly those hours, without hiring anyone full-time.

Book one waiter or several, for a single shift or the whole week. Every waiter is ID-verified by KvikStaff before they are sent to your business.

Common questions

How fast can a waiter reach me?

With an Instant booking, a nearby waiter can reach you in about 20 minutes when one is available in your area. If nobody is free at that moment, you can pick a scheduled time instead — for later today or tomorrow.

Can I book for one evening only?

Yes. The minimum booking is just 1 hour, so one evening, one lunch rush, or one full event is fine. There is no long-term commitment — book only when you need someone.

What if my regular waiter is on leave?

Book a KvikStaff waiter as cover for the day. If your regular waiter is away for longer, book the same days on a recurring booking until they are back — your service never has to stop.
