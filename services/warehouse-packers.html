About this service

Sale days and bulk orders don't wait. When parcels pile up, you need extra hands fast — not a week of agency calls.

KvikStaff warehouse packers join your team for the hours or days you book, and get straight to packing, sorting and labelling, so your dispatch keeps moving and orders leave on time.

What packers do — and what they don't

Clear work, agreed before the shift starts. No surprises on the day.

What's included

  • Packing boxes
  • Sorting products
  • Labelling parcels
  • Loading and unloading light goods
  • Stock counting help
  • Sealing and taping

What it's not for

  • Forklift or heavy machinery operation
  • Truck driving
  • Very heavy lifting
  • Inventory management or accounts work

Who books warehouse packers

Warehouses and godowns E-commerce sellers Wholesalers Shops with heavy dispatch work Businesses with seasonal order spikes
