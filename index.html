3 ways to book

An emergency, a planned day, or staff on fixed days every week — book the way that fits your business. Every booking depends on worker availability in your area.

For emergencies

Instant Booking

A nearby worker is available right now. Pick your time — say 10 AM to 1 PM — and he is on his way, usually within ~20 minutes. Instant bookings run between 8 AM and 10 PM and last up to 4 hours. If no worker is free, the app automatically shows you scheduled slots instead.

Plan ahead

Scheduled Booking

See worker slots up to tomorrow and pick one — or book directly for any later date. Book a single day or a date range (like 17–20 Sep) and skip the days you don't need.

Set it once

Recurring Booking

Staff every Friday, or every Mon/Wed/Sat, for a month or more. Skip Sundays, weekends or any off-days. Perfect when you need staff on fixed days — but not full-time.
