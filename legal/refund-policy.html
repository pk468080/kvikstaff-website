Refund & Cancellation Policy

Simple, fair rules for cancelling a booking and getting your money back — no hidden charges, no surprises.

Last updated: 20 October 2026

The short version

  • Cancel before a worker accepts? Full refund.
  • We could not fulfil your booking? Full refund, automatically.
  • Work never started? Full refund.
  • Refunds go back to your original payment method via Razorpay, usually in 5–7 business days.
1

Cancelling before a worker is assigned

If you cancel a booking before any worker has accepted it, you get a full refund. No questions asked.

2

Cancelling after a worker has accepted

If a worker has accepted your booking and is on the way, a cancellation fee may apply. The exact fee, if any, is shown in the app before you confirm the cancellation — you will never be charged a fee you did not see first.

3

After the worker has arrived

Once the worker has arrived at your location or work has started (start OTP entered), the booking is treated as used and the booked hours are not refundable, except in the situations below.

4

If we cannot fulfil your booking

If no worker accepts your booking, or the assigned worker cannot reach you, we will try to assign another worker. If that is not possible, you get a full refund automatically.

5

Worker no-show or work never started

If the work never started (the start OTP was never entered), you get a full refund.

6

Service quality issues

If something went seriously wrong during a booking, tell us within 24 hours on WhatsApp (+91 97590 49639). We will review the booking and, where appropriate, offer a partial refund or credit.

Please tell us within 24 hours of the booking so we can check what happened and help you quickly.

7

Scheduled and recurring bookings

You can cancel a single upcoming booking or a single occurrence of a recurring booking from the app before its start time. The rules above apply to each booking or occurrence separately.

8

How and when refunds reach you

Approved refunds are processed through Razorpay to your original payment method (UPI, card or bank account) and typically reach your account within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank.

9

Worker cancellations

Workers are expected to cancel only in genuine emergencies. If your worker cancels, we will try to assign another worker or refund you in full. Workers who cancel late or repeatedly may face account restrictions.

10

Contact us

For any refund question, WhatsApp us at +91 97590 49639.

Have a question about a refund?

Message us on WhatsApp with your booking details and we will check it for you.
