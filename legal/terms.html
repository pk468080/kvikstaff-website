Last updated: 20 October 2026

These terms apply when you use KvikStaff — whether you are a business booking staff or a worker accepting jobs. By using KvikStaff, you agree to these terms.

1.About KvikStaff

KvikStaff is a platform that connects businesses (“Customers”) with independent workers (“Workers”) for short-term staffing — event staff, waiters, pantry boys, office boys, housekeeping, delivery runners and warehouse packers.

KvikStaff provides the booking platform. Workers are independent service providers, not employees of KvikStaff.

2.Eligibility

  • You must be 18 years or older to use KvikStaff.
  • Customers must book staff only for lawful business purposes.
  • Workers must complete identity (KYC) verification before accepting jobs.

3.Bookings

KvikStaff offers three booking types:

  • Instant Booking — a nearby available worker.
  • Scheduled Booking — a chosen date and time.
  • Recurring Booking — repeating days, such as every Friday.

The minimum booking is 1 hour. The final price is always shown in the app before payment: it is calculated from the hourly rate and the hours booked, with duration discounts as displayed, plus GST when applicable.

A booking is confirmed only after successful payment.

4.Work start and end (OTP)

Work starts when the Customer shares the start OTP with the Worker, and ends with the end OTP. Booked hours run between these two OTP confirmations.

The Customer is responsible for sharing the OTP only when the Worker has actually arrived and finished, respectively.

5.Payments

Customers pay in advance through the app using Razorpay (UPI, cards and netbanking).

Workers are paid their earnings through the platform after a booking is completed, as per the payout schedule shown in the worker app.

6.Customer responsibilities

  • Provide accurate job details and a safe workplace.
  • Treat Workers with respect.
  • Ask only for work within the booked service type.
  • Be available (or arrange for someone to be available) for OTP confirmation at the start and end of work.

7.Worker responsibilities

  • Arrive on time at the booking location.
  • Carry a valid ID.
  • Do the booked work honestly and carefully.
  • Follow reasonable workplace rules of the Customer during the booking.

Cancellations and refunds are governed by our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

9.Accounts and conduct

Ratings and reviews must be honest.

10.Our role and liability

KvikStaff’s role is limited to providing the platform and payment facilitation. To the extent permitted by law, KvikStaff’s total liability for a booking is limited to the amount paid for that booking.

If you face a problem with a booking, report it to us within 24 hours so we can help.
