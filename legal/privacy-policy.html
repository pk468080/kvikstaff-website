Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

1. Who we are

KvikStaff is an on-demand staffing platform that connects businesses with verified workers — event staff, waiters, pantry boys, office boys, housekeeping staff, delivery runners and warehouse packers — through our mobile apps and this website. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, mobile number and email address when you register.
  • For business customers: business name, service addresses and booking details.
  • For workers: name, mobile number, photograph, date of birth, government ID / KYC documents (such as Aadhaar and PAN) for identity verification, and bank account or UPI details so we can pay you.
  • Location: customer service addresses to deliver the booking; a worker's location during an active booking, for tracking and safety.
  • Payments: payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not store your card or UPI details on our servers.
  • Device and usage information: device details, app usage and website analytics (including Google Analytics) that help us improve our service.

3. How we use your information

We use your information:

  • To create and manage your account
  • To match bookings with nearby available workers
  • To process payments and worker payouts
  • To verify worker identity (KYC)
  • To keep bookings safe with OTP-based work start and end
  • To provide customer support
  • To send booking updates through notifications, SMS or WhatsApp
  • To improve our platform
  • To comply with the law

4. Sharing your information

We share only what is needed:

  • With the customer or worker on your booking (such as name, photo, phone number and location, so the booking can be completed)
  • With Razorpay, to process payments
  • With service providers who help us run the platform
  • With government or law-enforcement authorities when the law requires it

We do not sell your personal data.

5. Data retention and security

We keep your information while your account is active and for as long as the law requires (for example, payment records). KYC documents are stored securely. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, though no system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information or account. Contact us on WhatsApp at +91 97590 49639 and we will act on your request as required by law.

7. Age requirement

KvikStaff services are for users aged 18 and above. Workers must be 18 or older to join.

8. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new "last updated" date. Continuing to use KvikStaff after an update means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact us

Questions about this policy? WhatsApp us at +91 97590 49639 or visit kvikstaff.in.
